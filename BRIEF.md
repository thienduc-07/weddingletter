## 1. The Eight Brief Questions

1. **Vibe in three to five words, plus references:**
   * *Words:* Romantic, tactile, intimate, regal, timeless.
   * *References:* Luxury European cotton wedding stationery with blush deckled edges; a sculptured champagne-gold wax seal; a cinematic morning light softly spilling over a garden wedding ceremony.

2. **The scroll journey, section by section:**
   * *Act 1 (Recognition):* The closed blush envelope arrives, resting gently above an ivory surface in morning light.
   * *Act 2 (Curiosity & Turn):* Scrolling turns the envelope in 3D space, revealing its layered paper folds and side thickness.
   * *Act 3 (Anticipation):* The envelope rests upright, back facing, displaying the sculpted metallic gold wax seal at the flap intersection.
   * *Act 4 & 5 (Delight / The Peak):* The visitor holds the wax seal for 1.5 seconds; the seal releases, the flap hinges open, and the exquisite invitation card rises out with a gold typography reveal.
   * *Act 6 (Awe & Immersion):* The scene expands into the romantic wedding garden world at golden hour with drifting petals.
   * *Act 7 (Intimacy):* An editorial love story album with asymmetric photographic frames and memories.
   * *Act 8 (Clarity):* Refined event schedule and venue details with interactive directions.
   * *Act 9 (Warmth):* An honest RSVP section honoring attendance and dietary needs.
   * *Final Act (Resolution):* A quiet, poetic close with settling rose petals and an elegant replay control.

3. **The energy curve:**
   * Starts peaceful and intriguing (floating closed envelope) -> builds continuous tactile engagement (scrolling rotation) -> focuses into deliberate tension (holding the wax seal) -> explodes into joyful payoff (envelope opening & card emerging) -> broadens into warm atmosphere (golden hour garden) -> settles into intimate reflection (couple's story) -> provides clear grounded facts (details) -> closes in deep serene intimacy.

4. **The feeling curve & The Peak:**
   * *Act 1 (Peace):* Soft morning light and drifting rose petals create an aura of quiet beauty.
   * *Act 2 (Wonder):* The tangible 3D envelope rotates under the user's hand, feeling physical and authentic.
   * *Act 3 (Anticipation):* The gleaming wax seal invites physical touch.
   * *Act 4 & 5 (The Peak - Joyous Revelation):* Holding the golden seal until it releases with sparkling light, followed by the flap opening and the golden invitation card emerging into the light.
   * *Act 6 (Awe):* The surrounding garden environment awakens with warm bokeh and botanical depth.
   * *Act 7 (Tenderness):* The editorial photographic story reveals the journey of two souls.
   * *Act 8 (Excitement & Readiness):* Clear ceremony times and venue instructions prepare the guest for the big day.
   * *Act 9 (Hospitality):* An honest RSVP invites their presence with care.
   * *Final Act (Graceful Contentment):* Soft parting words and the invitation to re-experience.

   **The Peak (The Tell-Someone Sentence):**
   > *"It's the site where you turn a real blush-pink wedding envelope around with your scroll, press and hold the golden wax seal until it breaks, and watch an exquisite gold-foiled invitation card slide out right in front of your eyes."*

5. **Signature Move:**
   * **THE GOLDEN SEAL RITUAL:** A bespoke hold-to-open interaction featuring a circular metallic progress fill, haptic feedback, specular light sweep, pressure depression, sparkle pulse, flap hinge animation, and 3D card extraction.

6. **Aesthetic Family:**
   * Luxury European wedding stationery: blush-pink paper, warm ivory ground, champagne gold foil stamping, sculpted wax seal, editorial Cormorant Garamond serif paired with Montserrat.

7. **Structure (One World vs. Distinct Scenes):**
   * Physical Artifact to World Transition: Begins as an intimate 3D object interaction on a pinned canvas, then naturally expands into an editorial wedding world and celebratory chapter layout.

8. **Assets:**
   * Pure CSS 3D geometry for the envelope, wax seal, flap, lining, and card.
   * High-resolution generated editorial photography for the couple's story and garden atmosphere.
   * Real semantic markup, vector SVG details, and responsive typography.
